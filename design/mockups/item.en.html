Item 1042 · Promise · Transport

Hélène Marchetti, 14 February 2020

Dès la rentrée 2021, les transports en commun de Valbourg seront gratuits pour tous les moins de 18 ans.

Translation from French

From the start of the 2021 school year, public transport in Valbourg will be free for everyone under 18.

Came true

Council decision no. 2021-047 made Valbourg’s public transport free for all users under 18 from 30 August 2021, two days before the deadline. The 2022 budget funds it.

Item 1051 · Promise · Housing

Daniel Okafor, 20 September 2022

We will build 400 new affordable homes in Port Ellery.

To be assessed

The deadline has passed. The town’s 2026 housing report counts 431 “below-market” homes completed during the term, without their rent or price levels.

What is missing

Stuck on the word “affordable”: the platform does not define it, and the town’s report uses its own term, “below-market”, without rent levels. Missing: the rent or sale price of the 431 homes, or the town’s written definition of below-market housing.
