# 0017. Top 100 predictions per person analysed first; everything extracted is kept; campaign promises are not capped

- Date: 2026-11-24
- Decided by: the founder, in chat (the cap, impact as a criterion, promises not capped); selection order proposed by
  the coordinator and accepted.
- Principles: 3 (one ruler — selection never looks at outcomes), 1, 5.

## Why

Some people make thousands of predictions (the off-record analysis of one blog found hundreds in a few dozen
articles). Analysing all of them is not affordable and would bury the important ones. So for predictions, at most 100
per person are analysed and shown.

## Scope

- **Only predictions published as commentary (时评)** — blogs, columns, opinion pieces, commentary programmes,
  replies under them: at most 100 per person (founder: "限制的只是时评发布的预言").
- **Not capped**: campaign promises (every recorded promise is analysed and shown, decision 0013), and every other
  kind of statement, such as forecasts made in an official capacity.

## Who qualifies

Only predictions whose outcome can be checked and that have a time range (decision 0015). For large events a clear
direction with a time range is enough. Vague ones are counted and the count is shown, not listed. Conditional
predictions wait for phase 2.

## Selection order (each step only breaks ties of the one before)

1. **Impact of the event**, by judgment with a one-line reason (no word list, as in decision 0016): world (global
   financial crisis, war between major powers, change of hegemon, pandemic) · national (change of government, an
   election result, a major policy, a country's economic course) · sector or region · minor (one company, one
   appointment, one meeting). At least half the places go to world and national predictions when the person has that
   many.
2. **Where it was said**: main channels (articles, programmes, interviews, speeches) before replies and comments.
3. **Specificity** (numbers, dates) — compared only within the same impact level.
4. **Spread**: one topic at most a fifth of the places; years roughly even.
5. **Revisions of one event count as one place**, shown together as a series.
6. **Remaining ties**: random draw with a fixed, published seed, so anyone applying the rule gets the same 100.

## Safeguards

- Selected before any verdict, without looking at outcomes; the selection records the version of this rule.
- The person's page says: "N collected; 100 analysed under rule 0017"; the rest are shown as counts only.

## Objections and risks

- Impact is a judgment; the reason line makes it checkable, and the founder reviews the selection.
- "At least half world/national" can favour sweeping, harder-to-check claims; the "checkable outcome" gate stays first.

## Amendment (2026-11-24, founder): a priority, not a cap

> 不是说是只记录一百条，是我们会按照我们的标准选出 top 一百，然后来优先的进行分析。其他的那些我们反正都已经抄录出来了，
> 可以把我们抄录的……而且我们初步的判断也都记下来。

- Every prediction extracted from commentary is kept: the verbatim quote, its source and our preliminary
  classification (checkable or not, time range, probability with its reason). Nothing is thrown away.
- The selection order above picks the **top 100 per person to analyse first** (evidence, jury, the founder's
  confirmation). The rest wait, recorded, and can be analysed later in the same order.
- Preliminary classifications are not verdicts. Whether and how the not-yet-analysed ones are shown on the site is
  decided with the design; they are never presented as checked.
