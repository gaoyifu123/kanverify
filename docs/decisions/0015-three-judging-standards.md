# 0015. Three standards for every judgment

- Date: 2026-11-23
- Decided by: the founder, in chat
- Recorded by: coordinator. They sit above every verdict rule (docs/methodology/verdict-standard.md); each rule cites
  the standard it comes from.

## The founder's words

> 我们在判断时，坚持三个标准
> 一，言论是金。我们正式要检查他们说话的可验证性，所以只要是公开发布的，后面发布的 contradiction 可以视为对之前的否定。事实此时不重要。
> 二，历史是变化的，是有周期的。承诺看他能实现承诺的能力期限（总统竞选承诺，对应的就是任期），预言是有一个时间范围。
> 三，common sense，看最终结果。

## In English, as applied

- **S1. Words are gold.** We check what people publicly said. A later public statement by the same speaker that
  contradicts an earlier claim counts as its negation; the facts at that moment no longer matter. Verdict: did not
  come true, and it stays so even if the result is delivered later. Exceptions kept: a vague or hedged claim stays
  unverifiable; a claim already fully achieved before the contradiction stays came true ("achieved once = achieved").
  This widens the renunciation rule of decision 0013's amendment from an explicit abandonment to any public
  contradiction by the speaker.
- **S2. History moves in cycles.** A promise is judged against the period in which the speaker has the power to keep
  it (a presidential campaign promise: that term — the inferred deadline). A prediction is judged against its time
  range.
- **S3. Common sense, the final result.** Claims and evidence are read with common sense, not legalistically, and the
  verdict is on the final result at the deadline.

## Open (asked in chat, 2026-11-23)

1. Does S1 apply to predictions too (a forecaster later saying the opposite = the prediction did not come true)?
   Applied provisionally to both, as one switch.
2. A prediction with no stated time range: give it a range (which?) or record it as unverifiable?

## Objections and risks

- "Contradiction" is wider than "explicit abandonment": more judgment per item. A postponement, a changed figure or a
  softer wording may each be read as a contradiction. The truth table will say what counts; S3 (common sense) decides
  the borderline, and the founder confirms every verdict.
- Only statements by the same speaker count; a minister or spokesperson contradicting a president is not covered
  unless decided otherwise.

## Answer (2026-11-23, founder in chat): a prediction without a clear time range

> 预言自己本身包含清晰的期限，否则视为太模糊。这和职务没关系，是关于世界运营的判断

A prediction must itself contain a clear time range; otherwise it is too vague (verifiability `vague`, verdict
`unverifiable`). No inferred deadline for predictions: unlike a promise, a prediction is a judgment about how the world
works, not tied to anyone's office. (Open question 2 closed; question 1 still open.)

## Answer (2026-11-23, founder in chat): S1 narrowed to an explicit, precise reversal

> 后期除非他明确准确的推翻当初的承诺，否则后面说的话都不当做对当时的否定。
> 言语是金，现在说的是金，当初说的是金。如果不是完全一致的话题，那么当做两个事情单独考虑。

A later statement negates the earlier claim only when it explicitly and precisely reverses that same claim. A
postponement, a changed figure, softer wording, or a statement on a topic that is not exactly the same is not a
negation: it is a separate claim, recorded and judged on its own. What is said now is gold, and what was said then is
gold. (This replaces the wider "any public contradiction" reading above and answers the objection raised.)

## Answer (2026-11-23, founder in chat): predictions are weighed one by one

> 一个人，可以关于一个事件，每个月都发表预言，修正。我们既要看他说准了没，也要看他发表了多少次，当初的放在现在是不是还是准的。所以，言语是金，我们要称一称

- A revision never negates or replaces an earlier prediction. Each prediction is a separate claim, judged on its own
  against the outcome. The reversal rule (RN-1) applies to promises only. (Open question 1 closed.)
- Later, not in record format 12 (coordinator, principle 5): show predictions about the same event as one series per
  person — how many were made, how many came true, whether the early ones held. This needs a way to link claims about
  the same event; it is built once predictions are actually being recorded (the first layer records promises).

## Answer (2026-11-23, founder in chat): achieved, then undone before the deadline

> 按"看期限那一刻、看最终结果"，判未成真。

The state at the deadline decides (S3, final result). "Achieved once = achieved" (decision 0013 amendment) now means
only that an early "came true" may be published before the deadline; it is provisional and the item is judged again
at the deadline, where the state on that day is final. The history keeps the early verdict. (Fable review 8,
finding 1.)
